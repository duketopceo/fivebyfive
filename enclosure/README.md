# Enclosure Specifications

## Physical Layout
- **Top Row:** 5x 1u MX Switch cutouts (14mm x 14mm square standard switch plate holes, 19.05mm center-to-center pitch).
- **Bottom Row:** 5x EC11 Rotary Encoders (7.5mm diameter shaft clearance holes, 22-25mm center-to-center pitch to accommodate knobs).
- **MCU Cavity:** Sized to fit Waveshare RP2040-Zero or Raspberry Pi Pico with USB-C port cutout.

## 3D Printing Recommendations
- **Material:** PETG or PLA+
- **Infill:** 25-40% Gyroid for structural rigidity and acoustic dampening
- **Layer Height:** 0.20mm (0.16mm for top chamfers/bevels)
- **Wall Count:** 4 perimeters minimum
